Nation Franchise

A country's whole history as a national team — combined value over time, FIFA rank, and every season

A "nation" is everyone whose citizenship is that country (dual nationals count for both; not an official call-up list). The two top-26 squads enforce a minimum shape of 3 GK · 7 DEF · 7 MID · 6 ATT and fill the last three with the best players left. FIFA world ranking is shown per season (historical data ends Sep 2024; the header shows the current rank). Pick a season row below to open that squad, or browse every nation-season.

Data via transfermarkt-datasets. National-team metadata (FIFA ranking, badge) via the same source's national_teams table.
