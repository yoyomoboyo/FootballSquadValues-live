What every number on the site means, how it's built, and an example

Every value here comes from public football data (see the footer). The money figures are Transfermarkt market values — transfer-market prices shaped by age, contract and demand, not a ranking of how good a player is (a legend can be worth far less than a rising prospect). And nothing on the site is a projection or model: the "peak" figures are built entirely from values that already happened. This page explains each column — hover any stat around the site for the short version, and click through to here for the full definition.
